🗺️ Your journey
📖 How to play
  • Each turn: walk once + one action (fight, ability, magic or heal) + 1 item. Dice decide everything!
  • 🧰 Equip a weapon, armor (it wears out — repair it!) and an off-hand (shield, torch, tome, charm).
  • 🥚 Hatch dragon eggs: Guardian (+HP), Fury (+damage) or Battle (fights with you). Train them to level 5.
  • 🎡 Spin the Wheel of Fortune with tickets or gold. 🏘️ In the village, walk up to people and talk to them to shop.
  • 🏫 In the future school: pass the classroom tests on every floor, dodge bullies, and sleep on Floor 5. Survive 5 days!
  • 🌟 God Power charges every turn: Divine Balance heals & revives everyone and smites all enemies.
