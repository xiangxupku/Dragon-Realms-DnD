DRAGON REALMS D&D
=================

PLAY ALONE (no Wi-Fi):   double-click  public\index.html   -> Story Mode
PLAY ONLINE TOGETHER:    double-click  start-server.bat on ONE computer.
                         It prints an address like  http://192.168.1.5:8080
                         Everyone on the same Wi-Fi opens that address in a browser.
                         Host: Online Co-op -> Create Room -> tell friends the 4-letter code.
                         Friends: Online Co-op -> type the code -> Join Room.
FULL SCREEN:             press F (or the Full screen button).

THE JOURNEY
 1. The Storm Galleon (SeaWings)  - start at the bottom, climb 3 decks, beat the SeaWing Queen
 2. The Muddy Pond (MudWings)     - mud slows you and blurs your eyes, beat the MudWing Queen
 3. Peaceful Village              - walk around, talk to people, shop, hatch & train dragons, spin the wheel
 4. The Scorching Desert (SkyWings) - heat, fire traps, beat the SkyWing Queen
 5. The Stronghold (NightWings)   - beat the NightWing Timekeeper to open the time portal
 6. Yorkson Creek Middle School (the future) - 5 floors a day: pass classroom tests,
    talk to smart students for hints, fight bullies, sleep on floor 5. Survive 5 days to win!

All students and bullies in the school are made-up characters.
Needs Node.js only for online play (https://nodejs.org).
